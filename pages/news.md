# Updates from *The Skymu Times*

## Users Of This Fork, Beware

*Authored by Sam (OmegaAOL) -- November 9, 2026*

WARNING to those who download or install the "SkyLarping" fork of Skymu (that seems to be wholly vibecoded. It adds native XP support, and ends up removing the Yggdrasil/plugin infrastructure along with turning the client from a multiprotocol chat app into an advanced SillyTavern/OpenAI API-compatible roleplay frontend). IT WILL DELETE YOUR SKYMU DATA! (After analyzing the repository I discovered [this code runs on application start](https://github.com/randomNinja64/Skylarping/blob/master/Skylarping/Classes/Migrator.cs))

Make a BACKUP of your Skymu appdata folder before running this fork. We are not affiliated with Skylarping or its developers and are not responsible for any issues you may run in to with SkyLarping. We do not know of any other forks but make sure anything you download off the internet is safe before running it. 

I personally do not endorse using fully vibecoded software on your local machine if the developer does not understand how it works. Not making any insuinations about SkyLarping. And no we will not try and take down the SkyLarping repo [like some other kinds of developers do to unwanted forks](https://www.reddit.com/r/palemoon/comments/pexate/pale_moon_developers_abuse_mozilla_public_license/) because we believe in the spirit of FOSS and do not think the devs have any malicious intent at all, far from it. It's pretty cool someone forked Skymu even if the fork is vibecoded. I look forward to the further development of SkyLarping and I might even use it myself for SillyTavern stuff.

## The Past Two Months

*Authored by Sam (OmegaAOL) -- October 17, 2026*

CONGRATULATIONS everybody on 1,600 Discord members! This is a real milestone, especially just two weeks after we reached 1,500 members. We've also hit 80 stars on the GitHub and around 13,000 downloads from our current repository. With that in mind, let's sum up the events of the last two months.

Skymu development has been extremely inactive, owing to the fact that we're all busy with school, college and work. Almost no major codebase changes or additions have been made in around two months. However, a big release is coming that will feature several advancements, among them simultaenous multiprotocol mode (being able to use your Discord, Matrix and Whatsapp accounts all at the same time from a singular UI) and a littany of bugfixes and incremental improvements, such as GUI support for deleting, editing, and replying to messages. 

A new developer joined the Skymu team: WowInceptionGood. He's responsible for the creation of the Skymu Nightly / Beta installation system and has been involved with Skymu development for some time before his addition to the team in an official capacity. On the other hand, nilFinx has retired due to severe and irreconcilable disagreements with the entire rest of the Skymu team regarding her vocal support of certain drawings of minors - which we absolutely refuse to endorse.

PatrickTBP has also returned to Skymu development in his full capacity as a founding member of the project after he and Omega reconciled following a misunderstanding and falling-out. Jim has also rejoined the project, reprising his former role as a developer. Things are now all rainbows and sunshine over in Skymu land, but development is going at the pace of molasses as you're aware: we could use some help... If you'd like to contribute, get in touch *or just file a pull request!* We appreciate you all!
